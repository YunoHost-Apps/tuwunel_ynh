This version, among others, add `ffmpeg` support for generating missing video thumbnails. Consider re-applying `tuwunel.toml` by setting `yunohost app setting tuwunel overwrite_config -v 1` prior to upgrade. This will unfortunately remove your local customization. Remember to revert the setting back to 0 to avoid restomping in the future!

As always see the plentiful changes in the [upstream changelog](https://github.com/matrix-construct/tuwunel/releases/tag/v1.8.3)
